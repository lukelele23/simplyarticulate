Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers SimplyArticulate Pro monthly and annual subscriptions purchased through Paddle, our authorized reseller and Merchant of Record.

Free trial and billing

Pro subscriptions include a three-day free trial. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your selected subscription fee will be charged when the trial ends. Your subscription then renews automatically each month or year, as shown at checkout.

Canceling your subscription

You can cancel through the “Manage subscription” link in your Paddle receipt or by visiting Paddle support.

Cancel before your trial ends to avoid the first subscription charge. For paid subscriptions, cancellation stops future renewals, and Pro access continues until the end of the current paid billing period unless a refund ends access earlier.

Cancellation alone does not request a refund.

Refunds

We offer a full refund of your first subscription payment if you request it within 14 calendar days of that charge. This applies to both monthly and annual plans.

Renewal payments are generally non-refundable, but you can request an individual review for an accidental renewal, billing error, or unresolved technical problem. This does not limit any refund or withdrawal rights available under applicable law or Paddle’s terms.

Requesting a refund

Visit paddle.net and select the refund support option. You can also email simplyarticulate11@gmail.com for assistance.

Include your purchase email address, transaction or receipt number, and a brief explanation. Never send your full card number or password.

Approved refunds are handled through Paddle. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment method and financial institution. Pro access ends when the relevant subscription payment is refunded.

Your consumer rights

Nothing in this policy restricts mandatory consumer rights, including rights relating to faulty or misdescribed services. Paddle’s Buyer Terms and Refund Policy also apply, and any additional rights they provide remain available.
