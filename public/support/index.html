Help and support

Contact simplyarticulate11@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.

Include the page address, your browser/device, what you expected and the exact error. Never email passwords, access tokens, API keys or recordings containing confidential information. Do not send audio unless support specifically requests it through an appropriate channel.

The microphone does not start

  1. Check the browser’s microphone permission for this site.
  2. Confirm the intended input device works and is not muted.
  3. Close other applications that may hold the microphone, then use “Check microphone.”
  4. Download any existing take before refreshing.

Feedback is unavailable

Sign in with Google and check the allowance message. Practice and daily-challenge analysis have separate daily limits. Recording, replay and download remain available without AI feedback. If processing fails, keep the original audio and follow the recovery message instead of repeatedly submitting new recordings.

Progress is missing on another device

Check that both browsers are signed into the same Google account and online. Audio stays local unless explicitly saved to your account. Download an important take before clearing site storage.

Account deletion

Open Settings, use Delete account and complete the verification shown. If a stage fails, stay signed in and retry to resume. Email us if you cannot access your account.
