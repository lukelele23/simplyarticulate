Start without an account

Choose Speech, Research or Debate, pick a difficulty, and set a speaking time from 30 seconds to five minutes. Preparation is optional. Record, replay and download your audio in your browser.

What signing in adds

Sign in with Google for account progress, optional cloud recording saves, and one practice analysis plus one separate daily-challenge analysis per day. A survey may provide a one-time extra practice analysis. Paid plans are not available for purchase.

Feedback you can act on

Analysis combines a transcript, delivery measurements and AI coaching. Read the suggested next step, then try the same topic with that change. An eligible initial practice analysis has one focused retry within 24 hours, up to 60 seconds. Daily challenges use separate rules.

An illustrative correction

Example, not a user result: “Your recommendation comes after a long setup. Try opening with ‘The library should stay open later,’ then give one concrete reason.”

Understanding the scores

The coach rating uses ten stars. Daily leaderboard scoring uses a separate 100-point rubric for relevance, organization, support and clarity. These are AI practice assessments, not qualifications or a measure of your overall ability. Scores can vary. Compare the specific correction with what you hear in your recording.

Difficulty and practice modes

Difficulty selects a topic pool, not a certified skill level. Speech is general speaking practice. Research offers another topic category with the same optional preparation controls. Debate topics include an assigned side. The separate Speech & Debate section provides Lincoln–Douglas and Public Forum clocks; tournament rules may differ.
