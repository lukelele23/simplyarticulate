Privacy and recordings

Updated October 14, 2026. Questions and privacy requests: simplyarticulate11@gmail.com.

Recording on your device

Microphone permission is requested when you check the microphone or start recording. Recording and replay do not by themselves request AI analysis. The browser can keep local audio recovery copies, settings, introduction preferences and account-specific completion markers. Closing a tab does not necessarily remove browser storage. Download important audio before clearing site data.

When you request AI feedback

Your recording is uploaded for processing. AssemblyAI transcribes audio. Google Gemini receives transcript and practice context for coaching; factual claims may be checked with Google Search. The hosted backend uses Supabase for authentication, account data, private storage and analysis jobs. These providers and hosting services process the requests needed to operate the service, which can include technical connection and error information. Avoid recording confidential information or other people without permission.

Saving and sharing

Scores and the next practice focus are kept with progress. Keeping full transcripts and coaching is a separate choice. Saving audio to your account is another separate action. A public daily-challenge entry can expose your public username and score; publishing your transcript requires its own choice. Other visitors can read material you choose to publish.

Retention and deletion

Temporary analysis audio and provider transcripts are scheduled for cleanup after processing. Background failures can delay cleanup while it retries. Saved account content remains available until you remove it or request account deletion. Account deletion runs in stages and may need to be resumed if a stage fails. Local recovery data on other devices is not remotely erased. Operational cleanup records and service-provider logs can outlast the visible content; contact us for questions about a specific request.

Your choices

You can use recording and replay without signing in, decline AI analysis, download audio, remove saved feedback or recordings, and request account deletion in Settings. For access, correction, export or deletion questions, email simplyarticulate11@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before disclosing or removing account data.

Advertising

The current application does not load the AdSense delivery script or show live ads. A Google AdSense account meta tag is used for site verification. If Google advertising is activated, this notice and any applicable consent controls must be updated before ad requests begin. Google explains advertising-cookie controls in its advertising policy.

Service providers

Provider processing also depends on the service configuration and agreements in use. See AssemblyAI, Google, Supabase and Netlify for their published privacy information.
